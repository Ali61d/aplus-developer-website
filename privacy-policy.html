Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy covers all games published by APlus Developer on Google Play, including Blocky Gems, Arrow Meadow, Rotate Rings Puzzle, Find the Difference Puzzle, 1945 Air War: Plane Shooter, Logic Grid Puzzle, Mahjong Solitaire: Tile Match, Cryptogram Word Puzzle, Stunt Bike Trial, Candy Mash Fusion, and Ball Drop Quest.

What our games collect

Our games do not collect personal information. Our games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and they do not require you to create an account.

Advertising

Our games show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device information, including your device's advertising ID, to show ads that are relevant to you. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: Google Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Our games may save your progress, scores, and settings on your own device only. This information never leaves your device.

Children

Our games are made for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through our Google Play listings.
